## 2026-06-21 — Clinical Competency Review (CBT / IPT / DBT)

**Reviewer:** Psychotherapist trained in CBT, IPT, and DBT.  
**Files revised:** `CORPUS_COST.md`  
**Rationale:** A clinician supervising or overseeing a clinical trial must have training sufficient to assess therapist fidelity, troubleshoot technique failures, and ensure patient safety for the intervention in question. Documents describing treatment procedures for modalities outside the reviewer's training scope were removed. Reference and formulation texts retained where they inform cross-modal clinical reasoning without requiring modality-specific procedural competency.
